# Metrics

## North Star candidate
Weekly evidence-backed product decisions.

A decision counts when a user opens a theme, reviews supporting evidence, and exports or marks the opportunity for action.

## Activation
Workspace activation =
1. Upload >=25 feedback items
2. Complete analysis
3. Open >=2 themes

## Product metrics
- Activation rate
- Time to first insight
- Weekly active workspaces
- Feedback analyzed per workspace
- Evidence drill-down rate
- Opportunity action/export rate
- 7-day retention
- 30-day retention

## AI metrics
- Feedback-type precision / recall
- Theme agreement
- Severity agreement
- Hallucination rate
- Uncited recommendation rate
- Human override rate
- Median analysis latency
- Cost per 1,000 items

## Research metrics
- Synthesis time before vs. SignalIQ
- SUS usability score
- PMF survey: "very disappointed" percentage
