# User Interview Guide

Interview 15–20 PMs/founders before major feature expansion.

## Current workflow
- Tell me about your role and product.
- Where does customer feedback come from?
- Walk me through the last prioritization decision you made.
- How did you collect evidence?
- How long did synthesis take?
- What tools did you use?
- What was frustrating?

## Prioritization
- What makes feedback important?
- Do you use frequency, revenue, severity, strategic fit, RICE, or something else?
- Tell me about a bad prioritization decision caused by incomplete feedback.

## AI trust
- Would you trust AI to cluster feedback?
- What would make you distrust it?
- Do you need citations to original evidence?
- What accuracy would feel acceptable?

## Closing
- If SignalIQ removed one step from your workflow, which one matters most?
- What would make it worth paying for?
- Can I test a prototype with you next week?
